About Curry Culture

Bringing Central Otago a taste of home-style Indian cooking

Curry Culture Indian Kitchen & Bar sits in the heart of Alexandra, serving tandoor-fired classics and slow-simmered curries alongside a full bar. Our kitchen focuses on freshly ground spice blends, traditional cooking techniques, and generous portions — from a quick weeknight curry to a full banquet for two.

Whether you're settling in beside the fireplace on a frosty Central Otago evening, or enjoying the outdoor courtyard in summer, we want every table to feel looked after.
